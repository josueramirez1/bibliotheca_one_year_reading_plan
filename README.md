# Bibliotheca One Year Reading Plan
This plan uses Volumes I, II, III and V to accommodate readers who have not purchased the Apocrypha volume with their set. I've tried to pair it with the BibleProject's One Year Reading Plan pdf where there are hyperlinks to their videos and articles for further study but it is completely optional and not needed to read Bibliotheca. A sincere thank you for the people at Bibliotheca, manufacturing partners, Spinner Buchbinderei, Peach Tree Publishing, the scholar team, and Adam Greene Lewis, for their time and energy to making this kickstarter project into a beautiful set for all readers. 

To get a copy for yourself visit their website: https://www.bibliotheca.co/
